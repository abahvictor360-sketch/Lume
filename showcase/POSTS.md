## Tips

- **Post order:** LinkedIn first (it reaches clients best), then X, then Instagram. Add it to Dribbble/Behance and your Upwork/Fiverr portfolio so it keeps working for you.
- **Lead with `lume-mobile.jpg`**: it stops people scrolling. Add the other two as extra images.
- **Make the link yours:** a custom domain (e.g. `lume.yourname.com`) or a project name in the Vercel URL looks more professional than `lume-topaz-six`.
- **Add a "Built by [Your name]" credit** to the site footer that links to your portfolio or LinkedIn, so anyone who sees the demo can find you.
- **Short screen recording:** a 15-second clip of tapping a hotspot, adding to cart and sliding to order usually gets more engagement than still images.
